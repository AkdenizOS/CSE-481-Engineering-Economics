# Midterm announcement — about midterm (vize hk.)

Source: instructor's Microsoft Teams announcement, supplied by Efe on 2 November
2026. The original posting date was not supplied. This announcement specifies the
format and rules, **not a question-by-question topic distribution or exam date**.

## Türkçe duyuru

Sınav 7 soru olup bütün sorular test şeklindedir. hesap makinası kullanmak serbest
olup paylaşmak yasaktır. süre 40 dk 'dır. faiz tablosu verilecektir. ekonomik kavram
ile ilgili bazı kelimelerin Türkçe karşılıkları soruların anlaşılması için sınavda
verilecektir. ek kağıt verilmeyecektir.

## English announcement

The exam consists of 7 questions, all of which are multiple-choice. Calculators are
allowed, but sharing them is not permitted. The exam duration is 40 minutes. An
interest factor table will be provided. To help you understand the questions, the
Turkish equivalents of some economics-related words will be given in the exam.

The Turkish announcement additionally says that no extra paper will be provided.
