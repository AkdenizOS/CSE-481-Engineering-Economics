# Week 7 — Interest Rates, Exchange Rates, and Budget Deficits

> Syllabus (TR): *Enflasyonun nedenleri. Faiz oranları. Döviz kurları. Bütçe açıkları*

**Previous:** [Week 6](06-money-inflation-unemployment.md) · **Next:** [Week 8](08-monetary-policy-productivity.md)

## Goals
- Connect interest rates, exchange rates and fiscal deficits.

## Key concepts
- Causes of inflation: demand-pull and cost-push.
- **Interest rate** = the price of money. Nominal vs. real (Fisher: real ≈ nominal − inflation).
- **Exchange rates**: appreciation and depreciation, and their effect on imports and exports.
- Government revenue, spending, and the **budget deficit**.
- How a growing deficit crowds out private investment.
- The role of investor expectations.
