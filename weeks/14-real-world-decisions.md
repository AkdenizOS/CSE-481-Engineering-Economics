**Previous:** [Week 13](13-engineers-in-business.md)

## Goals
- Apply the principles to the standard decision types.

## Key concepts
- Improving service or quality; new product development or expanding output.
- **Equipment and process selection** — choose among alternatives that do the same job.
- **Cost reduction** — invest now to spend less later.
- **Equipment replacement** — when to retire an asset still working.
- The fundamental principles of engineering economics, pulled together.
- This is where Chan S. Park's *Fundamentals of Engineering Economics* becomes the relevant source.

## Reading
