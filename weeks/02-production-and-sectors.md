# Week 2 — Consumption, Production, and Economic Sectors

> Syllabus (TR): *Tüketim ve üretim. Üretim faktörleri. Ekonomide sektörler. Serbest piyasa*

**Previous:** [Week 1](01-scarcity-and-choice.md) · **Next:** [Week 3](03-trade-and-markets.md)

## Goals
- Identify the factors of production and the sectors of an economy.

## Key concepts
- **Factors of production**: labour, capital, land, entrepreneurship.
- Sectors: households, business, government, international organizations.
- The circular flow of income between households and firms.
- **Free market** — prices set by supply and demand.
- **Open vs. closed economy** — with or without international trade.
- Illustrated with the Turkish economy and comparisons to others.
