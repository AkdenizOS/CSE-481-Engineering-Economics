# Week 4 — Backtesting Strategies

**Previous:** [Week 3](03-technical-indicators.md) · **Next:** [Week 5](05-time-series-with-pandas.md)

## Key concepts
- Signal shifting: a decision at time t cannot use the close of t+1.
- Compare against buy-and-hold, XU100 and a sector benchmark; report cumulative return, Sharpe ratio, maximum drawdown, win rate, benchmark difference.
- Include transaction costs and slippage; use train/validation/test or walk-forward periods; flag high returns from very few signals.
