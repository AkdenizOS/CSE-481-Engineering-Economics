# Week 6 — Money Supply, Inflation, and Unemployment

> Syllabus (TR): *Piyasadaki para arzı. Enflasyon. İşsizlik. Philips Eğrisi*

**Previous:** [Week 5](05-living-standards-productivity.md) · **Next:** [Week 7](07-interest-exchange-deficits.md)

## Goals
- Explain inflation's cause and its short-run relationship with unemployment.

## Key concepts
- **Money supply**; printing more money than output raises prices.
- **Inflation** = a sustained rise in the general price level. Quantity theory: too much money chasing too few goods.
- Measuring inflation: CPI. Nominal vs. real values.
- **Unemployment** types: frictional, structural, cyclical.
- **Phillips curve**: a short-run trade-off between inflation and unemployment; it breaks down in the long run.
