## Key concepts
- Project indicator groups: trend (SMA, EMA, KAMA), momentum (RSI, MACD), volatility (Bollinger Bands, ATR), trend state (Supertrend), cloud (Ichimoku), market structure (support/resistance), activity (volume).
- A technical event is a timestamped research event: record the market state and the forward return after 1, 3, 5, 10 days.
