# Week 5 — Living Standards and Productivity

> Syllabus (TR): *Ekonomik sistem nasıl işler: Yaşam standardı, verimlilik*

**Previous:** [Week 4](04-supply-demand-market-failure.md) · **Next:** [Week 6](06-money-inflation-unemployment.md)

## Goals
- Explain why living standards differ between countries.

## Key concepts
- Living standards are measured by output per person, not total output.
- **Productivity** is the fundamental determinant — a country's income equals what it produces.
- Raising productivity: physical capital, human capital, natural resources, technological knowledge.
- Why country living standards differ, and why the gap persists.
