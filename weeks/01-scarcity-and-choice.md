## Goals
- Explain scarcity and why every choice has a cost.

## Key concepts
- **Scarcity**: unlimited wants, limited resources. The reason economics exists.
- **Trade-off**: getting more of one thing means less of another.
- **Opportunity cost**: the value of the best alternative given up. The single most-tested idea in this course.
- **Marginal thinking**: decisions are made at the margin — marginal cost vs. marginal benefit.
- Efficiency vs. equity — usually in tension.
- **Incentives** change behaviour; policies that ignore them backfire.
