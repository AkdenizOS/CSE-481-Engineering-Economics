# Week 12 — The Engineering Decision-Making Process

> Syllabus (TR): *Mühendislik karar alma süreci. Tasarım alternatiflerini değerlendirme. Ürün maliyet ve fiyatı*

**Previous:** [Week 11](11-international-finance.md) · **Next:** [Week 13](13-engineers-in-business.md)

## Goals
- Evaluate design alternatives on economic grounds.

## Key concepts
- Where engineering economics starts: the course pivots from general economics to engineering decisions here.
- Meeting design goals under cost constraints.
- Evaluating design alternatives: cost, benefit, risk, time.
- Measuring product cost and setting price.
- What separates an economic decision from a purely technical one — the money and the time value of it.
