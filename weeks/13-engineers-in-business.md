# Week 13 — The Engineer's Role in Business

> Syllabus (TR): *İş hayatında mühendisin rolü. Sermaye-harcama kararları. Stratejik karar tipleri*

**Previous:** [Week 12](12-engineering-decision-making.md) · **Next:** [Week 14](14-real-world-decisions.md)

## Goals
- Classify capital expenditure decisions.

## Key concepts
- How capital expenditure decisions get made and who makes them.
- Large-scale engineering economic decisions and their time horizons.
- Strategic decision types: equipment/process selection, expansion, cost reduction, replacement.
- The engineer as the person who supplies the numbers the decision rests on.
