# Week 3 — Interaction, Trade, and Market Systems

> Syllabus (TR): *Bireylerin birbiriyle etkileşimi: Ticaret, piyasa rekabeti. Kapitalist, kumanda, karma ekonomi*

**Previous:** [Week 2](02-production-and-sectors.md) · **Next:** [Week 4](04-supply-demand-market-failure.md)

## Goals
- Explain the gains from trade and compare economic systems.

## Key concepts
- **Gains from trade**: specialization by comparative advantage makes both sides better off.
- Absolute vs. **comparative advantage** — comparative is what drives trade.
- The **invisible hand**: self-interested action coordinated by prices.
- When government intervention is justified: market failure and inequality.
- **Capitalist** vs. **command** vs. **mixed** economy.
