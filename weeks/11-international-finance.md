# Week 11 — International Finance

> Syllabus (TR): *Uluslararası finans. Euro ve dolar. Sermaye akışları*

**Previous:** [Week 10](10-markets-and-policy.md) · **Next:** [Week 12](12-engineering-decision-making.md)

## Goals
- Explain reserve currencies and capital flows.

## Key concepts
- Why the dollar and euro function as key currencies in international trade.
- International financial centres and what makes one.
- **Capital flows**: foreign direct investment vs. portfolio investment; hot money.
- The effect of dollar and euro movements on the Turkish economy.
- Balance of payments: current account and capital account.
