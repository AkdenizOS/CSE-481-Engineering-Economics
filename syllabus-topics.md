# CSE 481 — Official Syllabus Topics

The official Akdeniz syllabus ([`resources/CSE481-syllabus.pdf`](resources/CSE481-syllabus.pdf))
lists 14 economics topics, micro then macro, then engineering economics proper. The
2026-2027 instructor teaches the course around BIST 100 financial analytics instead
(see [`weeks/`](weeks/)); these topics stay here as the written-exam background and
for terms that follow the official outline.

The topics are two halves: **topics 1-11 are general economics** (micro then macro),
**topics 12-14 are engineering economics proper** — evaluating design alternatives
in money terms.

**How to study them**
- These topics are definitional — each lists the terms in bold with a one-line
  meaning. If you can define every bolded term in a topic, you know that topic.
- Tie every concept to a real example. The syllabus explicitly says the Turkish
  economy is used throughout. Inflation, exchange rates and the TCMB are in the news
  daily; use them. [TÜİK](https://www.tuik.gov.tr/) has the data.
- Watch the three computational bits. Most of it is verbal, but productivity
  formulas (topic 8), the production possibilities curve (topic 9), and the
  engineering decision methods (topics 12-14) are where the calculations are.

## Topic 1 — Economics: Meaning, Scarcity, and Individual Choice

> Syllabus (TR): *Ekonomi: Anlam ve özellikleri. Kıtlık kanunu. Bireylerin karar alma süreci*


### Goals
- Explain scarcity and why every choice has a cost.

### Key concepts
- **Scarcity**: unlimited wants, limited resources. The reason economics exists.
- **Trade-off**: getting more of one thing means less of another.
- **Opportunity cost**: the value of the best alternative given up. The single most-tested idea in this course.
- **Marginal thinking**: decisions are made at the margin — marginal cost vs. marginal benefit.
- Efficiency vs. equity — usually in tension.
- **Incentives** change behaviour; policies that ignore them backfire.

## Topic 4 — Supply, Demand, and Market Failure

> Syllabus (TR): *Piyasa başarısızlığı. Dışsallıklar. Piyasa gücü. Talep ve Arz. Piyasa dengesi*


### Goals
- Draw supply and demand, find equilibrium, and identify market failures.

### Key concepts
- **Demand curve** slopes down, **supply curve** slopes up. **Equilibrium** is where they cross.
- **Surplus** (price above equilibrium) and **shortage** (price below).
- Shifts vs. movements along a curve — a standard exam trap.
- **Market failure**: the market alone does not allocate efficiently.
- **Externalities**: costs or benefits falling on third parties (pollution, education).
- **Market power**: monopoly (one seller), oligopoly (a few). Both restrict output and raise price.

## Topic 6 — Money Supply, Inflation, and Unemployment

> Syllabus (TR): *Piyasadaki para arzı. Enflasyon. İşsizlik. Philips Eğrisi*


### Goals
- Explain inflation's cause and its short-run relationship with unemployment.

### Key concepts
- **Money supply**; printing more money than output raises prices.
- **Inflation** = a sustained rise in the general price level. Quantity theory: too much money chasing too few goods.
- Measuring inflation: CPI. Nominal vs. real values.
- **Unemployment** types: frictional, structural, cyclical.
- **Phillips curve**: a short-run trade-off between inflation and unemployment; it breaks down in the long run.

> Syllabus (TR): *Enflasyonun nedenleri. Faiz oranları. Döviz kurları. Bütçe açıkları*

- **Interest rate** = the price of money. Nominal vs. real (Fisher: real ≈ nominal − inflation).

## Topic 8 — Open Market Operations and Productivity Measures

> Syllabus (TR): *Açık piyasa işlemleri. TCMB. Girdiler ve çıktılar. Verimlilik formülleri*


### Goals
- Compute total, labour and capital productivity.

### Key concepts
- **Open market operations**: the central bank buying or selling securities to change the money supply.
- The Central Bank of the Republic of Türkiye (TCMB) and its tools.
- The three basic questions: what to produce, how, and for whom.
- **Total productivity** = output / all inputs.
- **Labour productivity** = output / labour input. **Capital productivity** = output / capital input.
- These formulas are computational — expect to calculate them.

## Topic 9 — Production Possibilities and Efficiency

> Syllabus (TR): *Üretim olanakları eğrisi. Azalan verimler kanunu. Tam rekabet. Kapasite kullanım oranı*


### Goals
- Read a production possibilities curve and apply the law of diminishing returns.

### Key concepts
- **Production possibilities curve (PPC)**: the frontier of what an economy can produce.
- Points on the curve are efficient, inside are inefficient, outside are unattainable.
- The curve's slope *is* the opportunity cost.
- Difference between **productivity** (output per input) and **efficiency** (no waste).
- **Law of diminishing returns**: adding one input while holding others fixed eventually yields less each time.
- **Perfect vs. imperfect competition**.
- **Capacity utilization rate** = actual output / potential output.

- [OpenStax — Ch. 7 (production, costs, and industry structure)](resources/books/openstax-principles-of-economics-3e.pdf#page=171)

## Topic 10 — How Markets Solve Economic Problems

> Syllabus (TR): *Görünmez elin etkinliğini bozan unsurlar. Para ve maliye politikası. İktisadi dalgalanmalar*


### Goals
- Distinguish monetary from fiscal policy and describe the business cycle.

### Key concepts
- What breaks the invisible hand: externalities, market power, public goods, information asymmetry.
- **Monetary policy** — the central bank, via interest rates and money supply.
- **Fiscal policy** — the government, via taxation and spending.
- Capital stock and its effect on economic growth.
- **Business cycles**: expansion, peak, recession, trough.
- **Three functions of money**: medium of exchange, unit of account, store of value.
- Specialization and the division of labour.
